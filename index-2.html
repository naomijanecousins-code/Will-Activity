About the prototype


Educational use only. Use fictional scenarios. Do not enter real clients, students' family information, or sensitive personal data.


Core legal checkpoint


The prototype teaches students to distinguish drafting from execution. Under Wills Act 1837 s.9, formal requirements include writing, signature/intention and attestation by two witnesses present at the same time, subject to the statutory wording.


Suggested sources


  • Wills Act 1837, s.9 — legislation.gov.uk
  • GOV.UK — Making a will: Make sure your will is legal
  • GOV.UK — Making a will: Write your will


Teaching model
Interview → issue spot → draft → challenge the draft → identify further investigation → reflect on execution.






Will Builder V2 is a teaching prototype, not legal advice or a professional will-drafting system. It does not determine testamentary capacity, undue influence, knowledge and approval, tax, beneficial ownership, family provision claims, probate outcomes, or validity of individual gifts.
